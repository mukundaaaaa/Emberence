Content Repurposing

One long video. Many short ones.

Turn the long videos you have already made into a steady stream of short clips for Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts and more. We find the best moments, reframe them and polish every clip.

How it works

Get more from what you have already made

If you have long-form content, it already holds weeks of short videos. We help you find them.

YouTube videos

Long videos cut into Shorts and Reels that lead viewers back to the full video.

Podcasts and interviews

Full episodes turned into highlight clips that people can watch and share.

Webinars, talks and live streams

Recorded sessions mined for their best moments, tips and quotes.

Courses and tutorials

Lessons turned into quick tips and teasers that introduce your work to new people.

What’s included

Everything between a long recording and a batch of clips that are ready to post.

Finding the best moments

We watch your full video and pick the segments that work on their own as short clips. You can also tell us which parts you want.

Reframing for each format

We turn wide video into vertical or square, keeping the speaker in frame, so each clip fits the platform you post on.

Captions and text

Easy-to-read captions and short on-screen titles, so each clip makes sense even with the sound off.

Strong openings, tight cuts

Each clip starts on its strongest moment, with pauses and slow parts trimmed, so people keep watching.

Consistent branding

Your logo, fonts and colours applied the same way across every clip, so your content looks like one family.

Delivered as a batch

All your clips arrive together, named and organised, so they are easy to schedule across the weeks ahead.

Who it’s for

Anyone with long-form content who wants to post more often without recording more.

  • YouTubers
  • Podcasters
  • Coaches and educators
  • Businesses with webinars and talks
  • Speakers and event hosts
  • Agencies managing client content

How it works

A simple, organised process from your first message to the final clips.

1

Request a quote

Tell us about your long-form content and where you want to post the clips. A few details are enough.

2

Get your quote

We check with our editors and send you a clear price and delivery date. Work starts after you agree and pay the 50% advance.

3

We find and edit clips

We pick the strongest moments, edit each clip, and review the batch ourselves before you see it.

4

Review and receive

You review the clips and can ask for up to 2 rounds of revisions. You receive the final files once the balance is paid.

How pricing works

We do not have a fixed price list, because every project is different. We talk to our editors about your content and send you a clear quote before any work begins.

The price depends on:

  • the length of your original video or videos
  • how many clips you want
  • the length of each clip
  • the style you want, such as caption style and effects
  • how many formats you need, such as vertical and square
  • how many videos you need repurposed, since monthly packages are available
  • your deadline

What we need from you

To give you an accurate quote and start quickly, please have these ready:

  • A link to your original video files on Google Drive or WeTransfer, since originals give the best quality
  • The platforms you plan to post on
  • Any topics or timestamps you want us to focus on (optional)
  • Your logo, fonts and colours, if you have them
  • A sample clip you like (optional), and your deadline

Questions you may have

How is this different from Shorts & Reels editing?

Shorts & Reels editing starts from raw clips and builds a short video from them. Content repurposing starts from a long video you have already finished and turns it into many short clips. If you have raw footage for a new short video, take a look at our Shorts & Reels editing service instead.

Can I choose which moments you use?

Yes. Send us timestamps or topics you want, and we will start with those. We can also pick the strongest moments ourselves, or do both.

Which formats do you deliver?

We reframe clips for the platforms you tell us about, such as vertical for Reels and Shorts or square for feeds. Please mention every format you need when you request your quote.

How many clips can I get from one video?

It depends on the length of your video and how many strong moments it has. We will tell you what to expect in your quote.

How long does it take?

Every quote includes a delivery date. It depends on how long your videos are and how many clips you want. The clock starts once we have your complete files, your notes and your advance payment.

Do you post the clips for me?

No. We deliver the finished files, ready to upload, and you post them from your own accounts. Your original content and the finished clips stay yours, as set out in our Terms & Conditions.

Ready to turn one video into many?

Tell us about your content and we will come back with a clear quote and delivery date.
