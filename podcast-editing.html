Podcast Editing

Episodes that sound clean and look sharp.

Audio-only or video podcasts, edited from raw recording to a finished episode. We clean up the sound, sync your cameras, tighten the conversation and cut clips you can share.

How it works

Audio or video, we edit both

Whatever way you record your show, we shape it into a finished episode.

Audio podcasts

Clean, balanced episodes, ready for your podcast hosting platform.

Video podcasts

Multi-camera editing with synced angles and a clear, steady picture.

Interviews and remote guests

Recordings from calls, mixed so you and your guest sound even.

Clips for social media

The best moments of each episode, cut into short clips for Reels, Shorts and more.

What’s included

Everything between a raw recording and an episode your listeners enjoy.

Audio cleanup

We reduce background noise, hum and echo, so your voice comes through clearly. Results depend on how well the recording was made.

Balanced levels

Even volume between host and guest, and a steady loudness from the start of the episode to the end.

Tight, natural cuts

We remove mistakes, long pauses and filler words, plus any section you ask us to cut, without making the conversation sound chopped up.

Camera sync and switching

For video podcasts, we sync every camera with the audio and switch to whoever is speaking, so the episode feels easy to follow.

Intro, outro and music

Your intro, outro, music and sponsor messages, placed where you want them and mixed at the right level.

Clips and highlights

We pick the strongest moments and cut them into short, captioned clips you can post to grow your audience.

Who it’s for

Anyone with something to say who would rather spend their time on the conversation than on the edit.

  • Podcast hosts
  • Interview shows
  • Coaches and educators
  • Business and brand podcasts
  • Panel and roundtable shows
  • Video podcasts on YouTube

How it works

A simple, organised process from your first message to the final episode.

How pricing works

We do not have a fixed price list, because every show is different. We talk to our editors about your episodes and send you a clear quote before any work begins.

The price depends on:

  • the length of each episode
  • whether it is audio only or video, and how many cameras you used
  • how much cleanup the recording needs
  • how many clips you want from each episode
  • how many episodes you need, since weekly and monthly packages are available
  • your deadline

What we need from you

To give you an accurate quote and start quickly, please have these ready:

  • A link to your recordings on Google Drive or WeTransfer, with a separate file for each microphone or camera if you have them
  • Episode notes: guest names, and any parts you want cut or kept
  • Your intro, outro, music and logo, if you have them
  • Any sponsor messages and where they should go
  • A sample episode you like, if you have one (optional), and your deadline

Questions you may have

Do you edit audio-only podcasts?

Yes. We edit audio-only and video podcasts. Tell us which you record, and we will quote for the right kind of edit.

Can you sync multiple cameras?

Yes. Send us the file from every camera along with your audio, and we will sync them and switch between angles. If each person has their own microphone, send those recordings as separate files, since this gives the best result.

Can you fix a poor recording?

We can reduce noise, hum and echo and even out the levels, but no edit can fully rescue a badly recorded episode. If you are unsure about your setup, send us a short test recording and we will tell you what to expect.

How long does an episode take?

Every quote includes a delivery date. It depends on the length of the episode and how much work it needs. The clock starts once we have your complete recordings, your notes and your advance payment.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds of revisions are included. Please ask for them within 7 days of delivery. Bigger changes that go beyond the original brief are quoted separately.

Do you upload episodes for me?

No. We deliver the finished files, ready to upload, and you publish them on your own hosting platform or channel. Your recordings and the finished episodes stay yours, as set out in our Terms & Conditions.
