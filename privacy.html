This summary is for convenience. The full policy below is what applies.

1. About this policy

This policy explains what personal information Emberence Digital (“Emberence”, “we”, “us”) collects through this website, why we collect it, who sees it, how long we keep it and what choices you have.

By sending a quote request, writing a review or sending us your material, you agree to us handling your information as described here.

2. What we collect

When you ask for a quote. The details you type into the quote form: your name, email address, phone number (optional), address (optional) and a description of what you need. We also record the date and time of your request and your IP address.

When you write a review. We send a private review link to clients we have worked with. For each link we store the client name we entered when creating it, and sometimes an Instagram or other handle. If you write a review, we store the display name you choose, your star rating, your headline (optional), your review text, and when you wrote or changed it. We do not ask for your email on the review page.

When you hire us. The footage, music, brief and messages you send us for your project, and records of quotes, payments and deliveries. Payments are made by UPI or bank transfer, so we see the payment details that appear on your transfer, such as your name and reference. We do not receive your card or banking passwords.

Technical information. Like most websites, our server may record standard technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We also keep short-lived records of failed attempts to open a review link or sign in to our admin area, so we can stop guessing and abuse.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your quote request and discuss your project.
  • To do the work you hired us for, deliver it and handle payment.
  • To invite you to review our work, and to show your review on our website.
  • To keep our website secure and stop spam and abuse. For example, we limit how many quote requests one IP address can send in an hour.
  • To keep records we need for accounting, tax and legal reasons.

We do not use your details to send you marketing emails or messages unless you ask us to. We do not sell your information or hand it to advertisers.

4. Who we share it with

  • Our editors. The editor and team members working on your project see your footage and brief. They are bound to keep it confidential and to use it only for your project. We do not share your email, phone or address with them unless it is needed for the job.
  • Service providers. Companies that help us run the business, such as our website hosting provider, email provider, and the file-sharing service you or we use to send footage (for example Google Drive or WeTransfer). They handle data only to provide their service, under their own privacy policies. Some of them store data on servers outside India.
  • Banks and payment apps. When you pay us or we refund you.
  • Authorities. If the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone’s safety.

5. What appears on our website

If you write a review, these appear publicly: the display name you chose, your star rating, your headline, your review text and our reply, if we write one. Your email, phone number, address, handle and private link are never shown.

Anyone can read a published review. You can edit or delete your review at any time using your private link, and it is removed from the site straight away. Please do not put private details about yourself or others in a review.

6. Cookies and third-party services

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run analytics tools on it. Our team’s private admin area uses a single sign-in cookie that is only for our staff.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which can see your IP address and browser details. Google’s own privacy policy applies to that.

If you reach us through Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, WhatsApp or another service, that service has its own privacy policy and we do not control it. If we add analytics or advertising tools to this site in the future, we will update this policy first.

7. How long we keep it

  • Quote requests (including the IP address saved with them): up to 2 years after our last contact with you, then we delete them.
  • Project files: deleted from our storage within 60 days after final delivery, unless you ask us to keep them or delete them sooner.
  • Reviews: until you delete them, or until we remove one for the reasons in our Terms & Conditions.
  • Failed link and sign-in attempts: about 15 minutes.
  • Accounting and payment records: for as long as the law requires us to keep them.

8. How we protect it

Our database is stored away from public access, our admin area needs a password, and we limit who can see your information to the people who need it for your project. We also limit repeated attempts to guess review links or passwords.

No website or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. If something happens that affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you,
  • correct anything that is wrong,
  • delete your information, and
  • stop using your information where you have given us your consent, which you may take back at any time.

Email us from the address you used with us and we will reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm it is really you first. If you ask us to delete something, we may still keep records the law requires us to keep, such as payment records. To remove your review, you can also use your private review link yourself.

We follow the data protection laws that apply in India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You may also have the right to complain to the authority responsible under those laws.

10. Children

Our services are meant for people aged 18 and over, and for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has sent us their details, please contact us and we will delete them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated. If we make an important change, we will make it clear on our website.

12. Contact us

For any privacy question, request or complaint, write to us at hello@yourdomain.com with “Privacy” in the subject line. Emberence Digital, Guwahati, Assam, India.
