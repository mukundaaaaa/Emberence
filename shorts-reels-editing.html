Shorts & Reels Editing

Short videos made to stop the scroll.

Fast, punchy edits for Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts and any other short-form video. We add the captions, pacing and sound that keep people watching to the end.

Vertical, caption-ready edits made for the Reels feed.

Facebook Reels

The same punchy format, framed and exported for Facebook.

YouTube Shorts

Quick, tight edits that work well on the Shorts shelf.

Any short-form video

What’s included

Everything that makes a short video easy to watch and hard to scroll past.

Hook and pacing

We shape the first seconds to grab attention, then keep the pace tight so viewers stay until the end.

Captions that pop

Accurate, easy-to-read captions in a style that suits your brand, so your video works even with the sound off.

Vertical framing

Proper 9:16 framing, with text and faces kept clear of the buttons and captions each app adds on top.

Music and sound

Cleaner voice, balanced levels, and music and sound effects placed to give each moment a lift.

Zooms, effects and transitions

Simple zooms, cuts and transitions that add movement without making the video feel busy.

Platform-ready export

Final files in the right format, ready for you to upload to Reels, Shorts or Facebook.

How it works

A simple, organised process from your first message to the final file.

1

Request a quote

Tell us about your videos through the quote form. A few details are enough to start.

2

Get your quote

We check with our editors and send you a clear price and delivery date. Work starts after you agree and pay the 50% advance.

3

We edit and check

An editor works on your video, and we review the edit ourselves before you see it. For most short videos, we aim for 24 to 48 hours.

4

Review and receive

You review the edit and can ask for up to 2 rounds of revisions. You receive the final files once the balance is paid.

How pricing works

We do not have a fixed price list, because every video is different. We talk to our editors about your videos and send you a clear quote before any work begins.

The price depends on:

  • the length of each video
  • how many videos you need, either one at a time or as a monthly package
  • how much raw footage each video has
  • the style you want, such as caption style and effects
  • your deadline

What we need from you

To give you an accurate quote and start quickly, please have these ready:

  • A link to your raw clips on Google Drive or WeTransfer
  • A short brief for each video: the message, the audience and the tone
  • Any reference reels you like (optional)
  • Your logo, fonts, colours or music, if you have them
  • Where you will post it, and your deadline

Questions you may have

Which platforms do you edit for?

Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts and any other short-form video. Tell us where you will post it, and we will frame and export it to suit.

How fast can I get my videos?

For most short videos, we aim to deliver the first edit within 24 to 48 hours. Your quote will confirm the exact delivery date. The clock starts once we have your complete footage, your brief and your advance payment.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds of revisions are included. Please ask for them within 7 days of delivery. Bigger changes that go beyond the original brief, such as new footage or a completely new style, are quoted separately.

Can I send footage I shot on my phone?

Yes. Phone footage is fine. Send the original files, not screen recordings or copies downloaded from an app, so the quality stays as high as possible.

Do you offer monthly packages?

Yes. If you post often, say how many videos you need each month in your quote request, and we will quote a monthly package.

Do you post the videos for me?

No. We deliver the finished files, ready to upload, and you post them from your own account. Your footage and the finished videos stay yours, as set out in our Terms & Conditions.
