If anything in a written quote we send you is different from these terms, the written quote is the one that applies to that project.

2. What we do

We provide video editing services for creators, brands and businesses, including YouTube videos, Shorts and Reels, podcasts and content repurposing.

Your project is edited by skilled editors who work with us. We manage your project from start to finish: we take your brief, give the footage to a suitable editor, check the edit for quality, and deliver the final video to you. Emberence is responsible to you for the quality and delivery of the work, and editors work under confidentiality terms with us.

3. Quotes and pricing

Every project starts with a quote. Prices are in Indian rupees (INR) and are usually a fixed price per video or a monthly package. A quote is valid for 7 days unless it says otherwise.

A quote covers only the work it describes. If you ask for more work later, such as extra videos, a longer video than agreed or new footage, we will send a new or updated quote before starting.

4. Payment

  • We need a 50% advance before we start work. Monthly packages are paid for each month in advance.
  • The remaining balance is due before we release the final, full-quality files. We may share a preview or low-quality version for your feedback before that.
  • You can pay by UPI or bank transfer. If we add other payment methods, we will tell you before you pay.
  • Please send a payment screenshot or reference, so we can match your payment to your project.

5. Your footage and content

You send us your raw footage, music and other material using a link such as Google Drive or WeTransfer. By sending it, you confirm that you own it or have permission to use it, including any music, clips or images in it.

We cannot take responsibility for copyright claims, strikes or takedowns that come from material you supplied. We may decline or stop a project that includes unlawful, hateful or abusive content, or content you do not have the right to use. If we decline, we refund any advance for work that has not started.

6. Turnaround time

For most short videos (Shorts and Reels), we aim to deliver the first edit within 24 to 48 hours. Longer videos, podcasts and monthly packages are given a delivery date in the quote.

The clock starts when we have your complete footage, a clear brief and your advance payment. Delays caused by missing footage, late replies or late payment move the delivery date by the same amount of time. If we expect to miss a date, we will tell you early.

7. Revisions and approval

  • Every project includes 2 rounds of revisions to the edit we delivered. Please ask for revisions within 7 days of delivery.
  • A revision means fixing or adjusting the edit against your original brief, such as trimming a cut, changing captions or fixing audio.
  • Changes that go beyond the original brief, such as a completely new style, new footage or a new video, are new work and will be quoted separately.
  • If we do not hear from you within 7 days after delivery, the project is treated as approved.

8. Cancellations and refunds

  • Before editing starts: you can cancel and we will return your advance in full.
  • After editing has started: the advance is not refundable, because an editor has already spent time on your project. We may agree to a partial refund if you cancel early and little work has been done.
  • If we cannot deliver: if we fail to deliver the agreed work, or we miss the agreed date by more than 7 days without a reason you agreed to, you can cancel and we will refund the payment for the work not delivered.
  • Refunds are paid back by the same method you used, within 7 working days.

9. Ownership and use of your content

Your raw footage stays yours. Once the project is fully paid, the finished edited video is yours to use as you wish.

We do not reuse, resell or share your footage or finished videos. We will show your work in our portfolio or on our social pages only if you agree in writing first. Saying no does not affect your price or service.

10. Confidentiality

We keep your footage and project details private. They are shared only with the editor and team members working on your project, who are bound to keep them confidential and not to use them for any other purpose.

We delete your project files from our storage within 60 days after final delivery, unless you ask us to keep them or to delete them sooner.

11. Client reviews

You can edit or delete your own review at any time using your private link. We may reply to your review publicly. We may remove or hide a review only if it contains abuse, spam, private information about someone else, or is not about our service. We do not remove a review just because it is critical.
