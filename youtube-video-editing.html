YouTube Video Editing

Long-form videos that keep people watching.

From raw footage to a polished upload. We shape your recording into a clear, engaging video with clean cuts, smooth transitions and a finish that matches your channel.

How it works

What’s included

Everything that turns a raw recording into a video worth publishing.

Cuts and pacing

We trim pauses, filler and slow moments, so your video keeps its energy from the first second to the last.

Transitions and motion

Smooth transitions and simple motion that guide the viewer without taking attention away from your story.

Audio and music

Cleaner voice, balanced levels, and background music placed so it supports what you are saying.

Captions and on-screen text

Accurate subtitles, titles and key-point text, styled to fit your channel.

B-roll and graphics

We add your B-roll, images and simple graphics where they make a point clearer.

Colour and finish

Colour correction and a consistent look, so every video feels like it belongs to your channel.

Who it’s for

Anyone who records good material but would rather spend their time creating than editing.

  • Vloggers
  • Educators and coaches
  • Commentary and gaming channels
  • Interviews and talks
  • Explainers and tutorials
  • Business and brand channels

How it works

A simple, organised process from your first message to the final file.

1

Request a quote

Tell us about your video through the quote form. A few details are enough to start.

2

Get your quote

We check with our editors and send you a clear price and delivery date. Work starts after you agree and pay the 50% advance.

3

We edit and check

An editor works on your video, and we review the edit ourselves before you see it.

4

Review and receive

You review the edit and can ask for up to 2 rounds of revisions. You receive the final files once the balance is paid.

How pricing works

We do not have a fixed price list, because every video is different. We talk to our editors about your video and send you a clear quote before any work begins.

The price depends on:

  • the length of the final video
  • how much raw footage there is
  • the style and level of effects you want
  • your deadline
  • how many videos you need, since monthly packages are available

What we need from you

To give you an accurate quote and start quickly, please have these ready:

  • A link to your raw footage on Google Drive or WeTransfer
  • A short brief: what the video is about, who it is for and the tone you want
  • Any reference videos you like (optional)
  • Your logo, fonts, colours or music, if you have them
  • Your deadline

Questions you may have

How long does an edit take?

Every quote includes a delivery date. It depends on the length of your video and how much footage you send. The clock starts once we have your complete footage, your brief and your advance payment.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds of revisions are included. Please ask for them within 7 days of delivery. Bigger changes that go beyond the original brief, such as new footage or a completely new style, are quoted separately.

How do I send my footage?

Through a Google Drive or WeTransfer link. You do not need to upload anything to our website.

Who edits my video?

Skilled editors who work with us and keep your footage confidential. We review every edit before it reaches you.

Who owns the finished video?

You do. Your footage stays yours, and the finished video is yours once it is fully paid. We do not reuse it or show it anywhere without your written permission. You can read the details in our Terms & Conditions.

Do you offer monthly packages?

Yes. If you post regularly, mention it in your quote request and we will quote a monthly package.
